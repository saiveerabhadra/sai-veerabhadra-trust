Request for Assistance

Support for those who need it the most

If you or someone you know requires support, you may submit a request to SAI VEERA BHADRA TRUST. We are committed to helping individuals and families in need across various areas including education, medical care, basic necessities, and disability support. Our team carefully reviews every request and strives to provide timely and meaningful assistance to eligible applicants.

Types of Assistance Available

We offer support across multiple areas to address the needs of our community

Educational Assistance

Empowering students with the resources they need to succeed academically and build a brighter future.

  • School fees assistance
  • College fees support
  • Books and study materials
  • Scholarships for deserving students
  • Higher education support
  • Accommodation assistance for higher studies

Medical Assistance

Ensuring access to quality healthcare for those who cannot afford it on their own.

  • Medical support for treatments
  • Free medicines distribution
  • Medical camp assistance
  • Health check-up support

Basic Needs Assistance

Providing essential daily necessities to families struggling with poverty and hardship.

  • Food and meal support
  • Clothes for individuals and families
  • Grocery kits and essentials

Disability Assistance

Supporting persons with disabilities with the necessary equipment and care they deserve.

  • Necessary equipment and aids
  • Rehabilitation support
  • Mobility assistance devices

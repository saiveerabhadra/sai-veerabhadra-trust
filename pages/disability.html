Our Commitment

SAI VEERA BHADRA TRUST aims to support persons with disabilities by providing necessary equipment and assistance based on their requirements and the resources available to the Trust. Our goal is to promote dignity, independence and inclusion.

How We Help

Our Support

We are dedicated to improving the lives of persons with disabilities through comprehensive support programs

Assistance with Necessary Equipment

We provide wheelchairs, hearing aids, prosthetic limbs, and other essential equipment to persons with disabilities, enabling them to lead more independent and fulfilling lives.

Support Based on Individual Needs

Every person's needs are unique. We assess individual requirements and provide tailored assistance to ensure each person receives the right kind of support for their specific condition.

Community Inclusion

We work to break barriers and foster inclusion by encouraging community participation, organizing awareness programs, and advocating for the rights of persons with disabilities.

Humanitarian Assistance

Beyond equipment, we extend humanitarian aid including medical support, rehabilitation services, and emotional counseling to help persons with disabilities and their families cope with challenges.

Request Disability Assistance

If you or someone you know needs disability support, we are here to help. Reach out to us and let us work together to make a difference.

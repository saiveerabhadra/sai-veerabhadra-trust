Celebrate, Give and Share Happiness

Turn your special day into a day of giving

A birthday is a special day to celebrate life, happiness and blessings. With Birthday Giving, you can celebrate your special day by sharing your happiness with people in need.

Instead of receiving gifts, you can invite your family, friends and well-wishers to support SAI VEERA BHADRA TRUST through a contribution. Your birthday becomes a beautiful opportunity to make someone else's life better, and every contribution you gather will go directly towards helping those who need it the most.

Your Birthday Contribution Can Support

Where your generosity makes a difference

Education

Support underprivileged children with quality education, school supplies, and learning resources to build a brighter future.

Scholarships

Help deserving and talented students continue their studies through scholarships and educational financial assistance.

Medical Assistance

Provide essential medical support, health camps, and treatment assistance for families who cannot afford healthcare.

Medicines

Distribute essential medicines to the sick and needy, ensuring everyone has access to the treatment they require.

Food Distribution

Fight hunger by distributing nutritious meals and food supplies to families and individuals living in poverty.

Clothes & Groceries

Provide clothing and grocery kits to the underprivileged, covering basic necessities of daily living.

Disability Support

Assist persons with disabilities with mobility aids, rehabilitation, and inclusive programs for a dignified life.

Skill Development

Fund vocational training, computer education, and job-readiness programs to help individuals become self-reliant.

Humanitarian Activities

Support a wide range of community welfare activities and humanitarian initiatives that reach the most vulnerable.

How Birthday Giving Works

Four simple steps to make your birthday count

1

Register Your Birthday

Fill in your details including your Name, Birthday, Mobile number and Email address through the registration form below.

2

Automatic Birthday Greeting

We will send you an automatic birthday greeting on your special day, wishing you a happy and blessed celebration.

3

Payment / Donation Link

A payment and donation link is included in the message, so your family and friends can easily contribute.

4

Celebrate by Giving

Your family and friends contribute on your behalf, and together you celebrate your birthday by giving back to those in need.
